ABOUT OUR STORE


Welcome to Kape't Balay, where every cup of coffee feels like home. Located in Calaca, Batangas, our café offers handcrafted coffee, delicious food, and a cozy space where friends and families can relax, connect, and create lasting memories. Inspired by the word "balay," meaning home, we are committed to providing comfort and genuine hospitality to every guest.
